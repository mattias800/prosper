refactor: move the texture-upload loops out of render_draw_pass_rgba

Two loops over the pass's `texture_uploads` vector, 286 lines between them, become
`record_texture_uploads` and `writeback_storage_textures`.

The first records every distinct upload into the command buffer -- layout barriers, buffer-to-image
copies, GPU detile, uniform clears, mip assembly from independent renderer targets, the borrowed
compute and depth sources, the colour-attachment feedback snapshot -- and reads nothing from the
pass but the uploads themselves, the command buffer, and the graphics shader-stage mask. The
second runs after the submit has completed and pushes each storage upload's staging bytes back
through the writeback callbacks the resource layer registered on it. They are kept adjacent
because the second is only meaningful for uploads the first recorded.

Verbatim moves; the call sites keep the conditions that already guarded them.

Both were unmovable until `SharedTextureUpload` left the function body: a 258-line loop with three
free variables could not be extracted while its element type had no name outside the pass. The
three free variables are not an estimate -- they are what the compiler named when the loop was cut
into a parameterless function, which is how the parameter lists in this series were derived.

  render_draw_pass_rgba: 6,006 -> 5,723 lines, 40.3% -> 38.3% of the file
